What you get

Included

  • 6 live group coaching sessions with Elevate You facilitators. You bring real situations; we practice responses together.
  • 14 short videos, one each week between live sessions, about 10 minutes, with a structured exercise to plan your week.
  • A weekly email every Monday with that week's focus, video and practice question.
  • Your Living Practice Workbook, a Word and printable workbook where you track your practice and progress. Yours to keep.
  • Three progress reviews at Weeks 6, 14 and 20 to look back at what has changed.
  • An optional private group for your cohort, with an email option if you're not on Facebook.
  • A recap and exercise if you miss a live session.

What you commit to

Your part

  • Join the six live sessions when you can (about 6 hours over 20 weeks).
  • Watch one short video a week and fill in a one-page game plan (about 20 minutes).
  • Spend 5 minutes each morning deciding where you'll practice that day.
  • Spend 5 minutes each evening noting what happened.
  • Practice in real situations you were already going to have: meetings, hard conversations, decisions.
  • As a founding member, share honest feedback along the way so we can improve the program.

What you work on

The outcome: catch yourself sooner, and choose a better response.

Each week you pick one specific situation and one response to practice, in a simple format: IF this happens, THEN I'll do this, HOW I want to show up. For example: IF I feel defensive when challenged, THEN I'll ask one genuine question before answering, because I choose curiosity over proving myself.

WEEKS 1–6 · FORM

See the pattern

Spot your recurring reactions, what triggers them, and how they land on other people.

WEEKS 7–14 · LOAD

Respond under pressure

Notice the pull earlier, choose a different response when it's hard, and recover when you slip.

WEEKS 15–20 · FLOW

Make it yours

Use it in your hardest situations and leave with a personal plan to keep practicing on your own.

Typical situations people bring: getting defensive, avoiding a hard conversation, saying yes and resenting it, overthinking, reacting badly when tired, and the gap between what they meant and how it landed.

Good fit

This is for you if

  • You know a pattern you'd like to change, but it keeps coming back under pressure.
  • You've read the books or done the workshop, and it didn't stick.
  • More people rely on you now, and your reactions matter more.
  • You're willing to practice for a few minutes a day.

Not a fit

This isn't for you if

  • You're looking for therapy or clinical support.
  • You want one-on-one coaching every week.
  • You want a video library or certificate to work through on your own.
  • You can't set aside the daily 10 minutes.

Questions

Why 20 weeks?

Changing how you respond takes practice in lots of different situations: a hard week, a difficult relationship, something new. Most of the 20 weeks is short daily practice, not coursework.

Who runs it?

Elevate You, co-founded by Matt Krall (former President & COO, executive coach) and Tamien Dysart. Live sessions are run by Elevate You facilitators; we'll introduce yours before you pay.

Who sees what I write?

Only you. Your workbook is yours, and you choose if and when to share anything.

When does it start, and how do I pay?

Early 2027. After you reserve, we'll contact you personally with the exact start date, all six session dates, the terms and a secure payment link. Your seat is confirmed when you pay.

Is there a guarantee of results?

No one can promise how a person will change. What we promise is the structure, the six sessions and the support to practice. What you put in shapes what you get.
