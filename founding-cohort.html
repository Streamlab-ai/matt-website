Why a founding cohort

The full 20-week experience, at a founding price.

This is the first cohort of the Individual Growth Cohort. Founding members get the complete experience for $900 instead of $1,500. In return, we'll ask for your honest feedback along the way, so it shapes the program for everyone who comes after you.

The learning introduces a possibility. The practice develops the capacity. This isn't 20 weeks of coursework. It's 20 weeks of real moments, at work, at home and in your relationships, used as practice.

What a week looks like

ONCE A WEEK

A short video and a game plan

About 10 minutes of video, then a brief structured plan for the days ahead.

EACH MORNING

About five minutes

Revisit your practice and picture where it might show up today.

DURING THE DAY

Practice in real life

Use the conversations and decisions you were already going to have. No extra appointment.

EACH EVENING

About five minutes

Notice one win, friction or insight, and learn from it. A slip is information, not a failed grade.

Six times over the 20 weeks (Weeks 1, 6, 10, 14, 17 and 20), the cohort meets live on Microsoft Teams for 45–60 minutes.

Three phases

FORM · WEEKS 1–6

See clearly

Notice familiar patterns, your intentions and their ripple on others, without judgment.

LOAD · WEEKS 7–14

Respond under pressure

Notice internal signals sooner, choose a response when it's hard, and recover after a slip.

FLOW · WEEKS 15–20

Make it yours

Take the practice into new arenas and build a system you keep using after the cohort.

Questions

How much time does it take?

About 10 minutes for the weekly video and game plan, plus roughly five minutes each morning and evening. The six live sessions are 45–60 minutes each.

Why 20 weeks?

Because practice needs varied real-life moments: a hard week, a difficult relationship, a new setting. The time belongs to practicing, not to consuming content.

Is this therapy or one-on-one coaching?

No. It's a facilitated group practice. It isn't clinical support and doesn't include ongoing one-on-one coaching.

Who sees what I write?

Only you. Your workbook belongs to you, and you choose if and when to share anything.

When does it start?

Early 2027. We'll confirm the start date and all six live session dates with you before you pay.
