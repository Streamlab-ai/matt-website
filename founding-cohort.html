The problem

You already know what you should do. Under pressure, you do the old thing anyway.

“I defend before I really listen.”

“I keep putting off that conversation.”

“I say yes, then resent it.”

“When I'm tired, I'm not myself.”

“I didn't mean for it to land that way.”

“The workshop made sense. Nothing stuck.”

Most leadership training happens in a room, for a day. The moments that matter happen later, in a meeting, an email, a hard conversation, when the training is long gone. That's why insight rarely turns into a different response.

Our approach

Short lessons. Long practice. In your real week.

Instead of more content, you get 20 weeks of structured practice. Each week you pick one real situation and one response to try, written as a simple plan:

IF

I feel defensive when someone challenges me…

THEN

I'll ask one genuine question before I answer…

HOW

because I choose curiosity over proving myself.

You try it during your day, note what happened that evening, and adjust. Six times, you bring what you've learned to a live coaching session with your cohort.

Questions

Why 20 weeks? That sounds long.

It's 20 weeks of practice, not 20 weeks of classes. Change sticks when you try it across many real situations: a busy week, a tense relationship, something new. Most weeks take about 1.5 hours, in short pieces.

What if I miss a live session?

You'll get a recap and a guided exercise so you can keep up. Sessions aren't published, to protect what people share.

Who sees what I write?

Only you. Your workbook is yours. You choose if and when to share anything.

Is this therapy or one-on-one coaching?

No. It's facilitated group coaching and practice. It isn't clinical support and doesn't include weekly one-on-one coaching.

Can my employer pay?

Often, yes. Many companies cover leadership development. Reserve, and we'll send you a short note you can share with your manager.

When does it start, and when do I pay?

Early 2027. After you reserve, we'll send the exact start date, all six session dates and the terms, including refunds. You pay $900 by secure link only once you've seen them. Seats go in the order people pay.
